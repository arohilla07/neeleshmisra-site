Artists, actors, musicians, writers and founders, speaking not as public figures but as people. Nobody is watching the time.

The clock in the room runs slow on purpose.

  1. No rush.An episode lasts as long as the conversation does. Most run past two hours. A few go close to four.
  2. No clock.No segments, no lightning rounds, no "one last question".
  3. No gotcha.Nobody is trying to trip anyone. People say what they came to say.
