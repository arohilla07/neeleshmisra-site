'''Neelesh Misra''' (born 4 May 1973) is an Indian journalist, author, radio storyteller,  scriptwriter and lyricist.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Lakshmi |first=Rama |date=2023-04-10 |title=He's the Garrison Keillor of India, and he's reviving the art of storytelling |language=en-US |newspaper=Washington Post |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/asia_pacific/hes-the-garrison-keillor-of-india-and-hes-reviving-the-art-of-storytelling/2015/06/29/348d5740-18d9-11e5-bed8-1093ee58dad0_story.html |access-date=2023-10-31 |issn=0190-8286}}</ref> He hosted [[Yaadon Ka Idiot Box|Yaadon Ka IdiotBox]] on [[BIG FM 92.7]]. He is co-founder and editor of Gaon Connection, a rural newspaper.<ref>{{Cite web |last=gaon |title=About Gaon Connection |url=https://www.gaonconnection.com/about-us |access-date=2023-10-31 |website=www.gaonconnection.com |language=hi |archive-date=31 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231031163407/https://www.gaonconnection.com/about-us |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-12-22 |title=Gaon Connection Survey: 50% rural Indians prefer India-made COVID vaccine |url=https://www.cnbctv18.com/videos/healthcare/gaon-connection-survey-50-rural-indians-prefer-india-made-covid-vaccine-7801411.htm |access-date=2023-10-31 |website=cnbctv18.com |language=en}}</ref> He is working on "The Neelesh Misra Show" on [[Red FM 93.5]] and "Kahaani Express" on [[Saavn]].

== Early life and education==
Misra was born on 4 May 1973 to [[Shiva Balak Misra]] and Nirmala Misra. He was born in [[Lucknow]] and brought up in [[Nainital]], [[Uttarakhand]].<ref name=ibn08>{{cite news |title=Next Big Thing: Meet Neelesh Misra |url=http://ibnlive.in.com/news/next-big-thing-neelesh-misra-scribe-turns-to-showbiz/66331-8.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110319165359/http://ibnlive.in.com/news/next-big-thing-neelesh-misra-scribe-turns-to-showbiz/66331-8.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=19 March 2011 |publisher=IBN LIVE |date=2 June 2008 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |date=2012-12-09 |title=Tapping the rural news space |language=en-IN |work=The Hindu |url=https://www.thehindu.com/todays-paper/ |access-date=2023-10-31 |issn=0971-751X}}</ref>

==Career==
As a journalist, he has covered conflict and insurgency over the past two decades in South Asia, traveling deep into the [[hinterland]].{{Citation needed|date= March 2018}} His travels have taken him from the rebel heartlands of [[Kashmir]] to [[Naxalite]]-dominated areas of central and eastern India, to the faraway north-east which is home to some of the world's longest-running insurgencies. For his reportage from India's insurgency lands, he received the [[Ramnath Goenka Excellence in Journalism Awards|Ramnath Goenka Award for Excellence in Journalism]]<ref name=":0">{{cite web|last=Express|first=Indian|title=Ram Nath Goenka Award for Excellence in Journalism he has been editor of Hindustan news paper|url=http://expressindia.indianexpress.com/ramnath-goenka-foundation/2007_winners.html|access-date=31 January 2014|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130624120717/http://expressindia.indianexpress.com/ramnath-goenka-foundation/2007_winners.html|archive-date=24 June 2013|df=dmy-all}}</ref> and the [[Karpoor Chandra Kulish|K.C. Kulish]] Memorial Award in the year 2009.

He has written five books, including ''The Absent State'' (2010), which he co-wrote with [[Rahul Pandita]]<ref>{{cite news |title=Book Review: The art of losing a war |author=Sudhir Mishra |url=http://www.hindustantimes.com/News-Feed/Books/The-art-of-losing-a-war/Article1-601491.aspx |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130125084307/http://www.hindustantimes.com/News-Feed/Books/The-art-of-losing-a-war/Article1-601491.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-date=25 January 2013 |publisher=Hindustan Times |date=17 September 2010 }}</ref>

Neelesh started his career as a Bollywood lyricist when while researching for a book in Mumbai, he met director [[Mahesh Bhatt]], which led to his debut song ''Jaadu Hai Nasha Hai'' for ''[[Jism (2003 film)|Jism]]'' (2003),<ref name=ibn08/> this was followed by hit songs like ''Kya Mujhe Pyaar Hai'' for ''[[Woh Lamhe]]'' (2006) and went on write over 20 songs in over 15 films.<ref name=hinnine11/><ref>{{cite news |title=I was offered to script film on Benazir Bhutto: Neelesh Misra |url=http://www.hindu.com/thehindu/holnus/009200902070922.htm |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130125123055/http://www.hindu.com/thehindu/holnus/009200902070922.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=25 January 2013 |work=The Hindu |date=7 February 2009 }}</ref> In 2012, he co-wrote the screenplay for [[Salman Khan]] starrer, ''[[Ek Tha Tiger]]'', along with film's director Kabir Khan.<ref name=ibn12/>

He was the creative director, singer and songwriter in the India's first writer-led band Called ''Nine'', with singer [[Shilpa Rao]] and composer Amartya Rahut, working with traditional Indian craft He is also a blogger.<ref>{{cite web|author=India |url=http://www.blogger.com/profile/12286847257521253623 |title=User Profile: Writer At Large |publisher=Blogger |access-date=10 August 2014}}</ref> His blog contains his experiences during his days as senior roving editor, his thoughts on various issues, and the poems he has written. He hosts a radio show, ''Yadoon Ka Idiotbox'' on [[BIG FM 92.7]], set in fictitious small town, '' Yaad Sheher'', and which started its second season in 2012. He also hosts " The Neelash Mishra" show on 93.5 RED FM.<ref name=out>{{cite web |title=Neelesh Misra: The journalist, lyricist and author on reinventing the art of storytelling|url=http://www.outlookindia.com/article.aspx?282256 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120915232350/http://outlookindia.com/article.aspx?282256 |url-status=dead |archive-date=15 September 2012 |date=24 September 2012|publisher=Outlook }}</ref><ref name=ibn12/> In late 2012, he along with Karan Dalal, started a rural newspaper, ''Gaon Connection'', based in Kunaura, a village near Lucknow.<ref name=hindudec12>{{cite news |title=Tapping the rural news space |url=https://www.thehindu.com/todays-paper/tp-features/tapping-the-rural-news-space/article4183089.ece |work= The Hindu|date=10 December 2012 }}</ref><ref name="gaonconnection.com">{{cite web |last=Beuro |first=Gaon Connection |title=Gaon Connection: Agriculture & Rural News, News in Hindi, हिन्दी न्यूज |url=https://www.gaonconnection.com/ |website=www.gain connection.com}}</ref>

==Personal life==
Misra married [[Nidhi Razdan]], a news anchor and editor, in 2005.<ref name=ig-romance>{{cite web|url=http://www.indiaglitz.com/channels/hindi/interview/6484.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060505222853/http://www.indiaglitz.com/channels/hindi/interview/6484.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=5 May 2006|title=Actual romance blooms in small towns: Neelesh Misra|website=IndiaGlitz.com|date=29 November 2005|access-date=26 November 2014}}</ref><ref name=nidhi-bio>{{cite web|url=http://www.in.com/nidhi-razdan/biography-208665.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121112141700/http://www.in.com/nidhi-razdan/biography-208665.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=12 November 2012|title=Nidhi Razdan|publisher=in.com|access-date=26 November 2014}}</ref> The couple divorced in 2007.

Misra married Yamini Misra in 2008 and they had a daughter. Yamini also writes some stories for the Neelesh Misra Show.<ref name="it-gaon-connection">{{cite web|url=http://www.indiantelevision.com/special/y2k13/Gaon_connection_special.php|title=Gaon Connection: Going beyond the village|publisher=indiantelevision.com|date=2 July 2013|access-date=26 November 2014}}</ref>
