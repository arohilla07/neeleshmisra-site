# The Slow Interview: show facts (fetched 2026-11-05)

Everything below comes from the staging WordPress (imported neeleshmisra.com content), the live neeleshmisra.com listing, and the @NeeleshMisra YouTube channel, its playlists and video descriptions. Where a source doesn't say something, this file doesn't either.

## Start
- The About page says: "In 2018, Neelesh Misra launched The Slow Interview on YouTube." It describes the format as having no time limits, no viral hooks and no promotional agendas, with conversations "often lasting over an hour". Source: https://midnightblue-crocodile-355322.hostingersite.com/about-us/
- The first uploads are five short Pankaj Tripathi clips (2018-11-18 to 11-22, e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PeH_7oZRimI), then "Pankaj Tripathi | Episode 1" on 2018-11-23: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kl5Sku5FFu0. The staging site titles that post "The Interview that started it all".

## Related series
- **A Slow Day with Neelesh Misra**: the live site's Slow Interview page describes it as a new conversation series, after The Slow Interview, about "business, ambition, leadership and ideas". The first episode features Aditya Ghosh (co-founder of Akasa Air, former Indigo, former CEO of OYO). Full episode on 2026-05-16: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vX-E3Dp-oCM. Playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLRknjC5MPHa3-nDgizYkCVu7eAmbPfUm1. Live page: https://neeleshmisra.com/watch/the-slow-interview
- **The Slow Cafe**: several S2/S3 descriptions (Manav Kaul, Suresh Raina, Naezy, Ali Fazal) open with "Presenting a very special episode of The Slow Cafe with Neelesh Misra". The channel also has "Slow Moments | TSC with Neelesh Misra" clips (Yami Gautam, Varun Grover, Bhumi Pednekar and others), which look like a separate show. Those clips are not in the JSON.
- **The Slow Interview Stories** playlist (46 "Slow Moments | TSI" clips): https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLRknjC5MPHa22PFoAhM0u6SiOGHgB20Iq. Not in the JSON.
- **The Slow Spoof** (2021-12-31), a parody of the show: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eFSxJVVTwwo. In the JSON as series "Other".

- I found no named sponsor for any Slow Interview season in the descriptions or on the site.

## Where it is filmed
No source names a fixed studio or location. The only clues:
